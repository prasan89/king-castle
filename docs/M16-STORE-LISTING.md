**King Smash — Launch. Destroy. Rescue.**

The evil forces have kidnapped the Queen and locked her deep inside their fortified castles. Only one king has the courage — and the raw destructive power — to smash through every wall and bring her home.

That king is YOU.

**HOW TO PLAY**
Aim your King at enemy castles and launch! Physics-based destruction does the rest. Smash towers, topple walls, and clear every last enemy block to rescue the Queen. The more you destroy, the more stars you earn.

**100 LEVELS ACROSS 5 EPIC WORLDS**
- 🏔️ Rocky Highlands — learn the basics and master the arc
- 🌋 Volcano Keep — crumbling terrain and explosive hazards
- 🌊 Coastal Fortress — water physics and slippery surfaces
- 🌙 Shadow Citadel — dark structures with hidden weak points
- 👑 Royal Palace — the ultimate confrontation

Each world brings new castle designs, harder enemies, and fresh physics surprises. No two levels play the same.

**UPGRADE YOUR KING**
Spend coins to boost your King's four core stats:
- **Power** — hit harder, break tougher blocks
- **Weight** — push through obstacles without slowing
- **Bounce** — ricochet for multi-target chaos
- **Range** — reach distant towers with precision

Find the build that matches your playstyle and dominate every level.

**5 POWER-UPS**
When ordinary destruction isn't enough, power-ups change everything:
- **Thunder Strike** — split into three for triple damage
- **Shield Breaker** — punch through reinforced walls instantly
- **Magnetic Pull** — drag loose blocks into chain reactions
- **Frost Blast** — freeze the castle before impact
- **Royal Rage** — double your launch power for one devastating shot

**FEATURES**
- 100 hand-crafted levels with 3-star scoring
- Physics-based destruction — every level is destroyable in multiple ways
- 4 upgradeable King stats for deep progression
- 5 unique power-ups collectible throughout gameplay
- 5 distinct worlds with different visual themes and physics environments
- Daily missions and achievement system
- Cloud save via Google Play — continue across devices
- Optional Google Sign-In for cross-device progress sync
- Smooth performance optimized for a wide range of Android devices

**FREE TO PLAY**
King Smash is free to download and play. Coins are earned naturally through gameplay. Optional in-app purchases are available for players who want to progress faster or remove ads. All game content is accessible without purchasing.

### Icon Design Guidance

- Show the King character prominently — face or full body
- Include castle silhouette as background context
- Use bold, high-contrast colors — readable at small sizes (48 px launcher)
- Game title on icon is optional; Play Console displays app name separately
- Do not use: competitor IP, misleading representations of a different game style, Google Play badge imagery
- Test at 48 px, 96 px, and 192 px to verify readability at launcher and store sizes

| Nudity | NO | — |
| Sexual content | NO | — |
| Language | NO | No profanity |
| User-generated content | NO | No UGC features |
| Location sharing | NO | — |
| Digital purchases | YES | IAP (coins, gems, remove-ads) |
| Advertising | YES | AdMob rewarded and interstitial |
| Users interact | NO | No social/chat features |

### Expected Rating

**Everyone (E)** — most likely outcome for a cartoon physics castle game with no blood, no social features, and mild-only violence.

**Everyone 10+ (E10+)** — possible if IARC scores the "mild cartoon violence" higher than expected. This does not affect visibility for the target audience.

Rating is jurisdiction-specific. PEGI (EU) equivalent: **PEGI 3** or **PEGI 7**.

## Soft-Launch Metadata Notes

- **Placeholder assets**: Placeholder icon and screenshots are acceptable for Internal Testing and Closed Testing tracks. Do not submit placeholder assets to Production.
- **Trademark**: "King Smash" — verify trademark availability in all target markets before production launch. Conduct search via USPTO (US), EUIPO (EU), and local IP offices.
- **Developer name**: The developer name visible on the store listing is tied to your Play Console account. It cannot be changed per-app and is difficult to change retroactively. Set the legal entity name before first public release.
- **Package name**: `com.kingcastle.kingsmash` is permanent once published. If incorrect, a new app listing is required. Verify before first production release.
- **Version naming**: Use semantic versioning (e.g., 1.0.0 for launch, 1.0.1 for patches). Version name is user-visible; version code is internal-only but must always increment.
- **Rating localization**: IARC rating applies globally. Some markets (South Korea, Brazil) require separate local ratings — plan for these before expanding to those markets.
