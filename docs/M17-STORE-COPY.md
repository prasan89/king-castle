**King Smash — Launch. Destroy. Rescue.**

The evil forces have kidnapped the Queen and locked her deep inside their fortified castles. Every wall is reinforced. Every guard is ready. The towers stretch high and the gates hold fast.

Only one king has the power — and the courage — to smash through every last brick and bring her home.

That king is you.

**HOW TO PLAY**

Draw back, aim your King at the enemy castle, and let go. Physics-based destruction takes over from there. Towers crack, walls crumble, guard blocks scatter across the battlefield. Line up the perfect trajectory, chain one collapse into the next, and clear the level to rescue the Queen. The more you destroy, the more stars you earn.

Every level is a physics puzzle. Every shot matters.

**100 LEVELS ACROSS 5 EPIC WORLDS**

Each world brings new castle designs, harder enemies, and fresh physics surprises. No two levels play the same way.

• Forest Kingdom (Levels 1–20) — Rolling hills and timber strongholds. Learn the fundamentals of trajectory and angle. Wooden walls fall fast, but stone foundations hold their ground.

• Desert Fortress (Levels 21–40) — Sandstone walls bake under a scorching sun. Crumbling terrain shifts mid-level. Guard formations change. The architecture gets taller and the windows narrower.

• Volcanic Wasteland (Levels 41–60) — The ground itself is unstable. Lava-hardened blocks resist ordinary impacts. Chain the collapses right and the terrain does half the work for you.

• Arctic Citadel (Levels 61–80) — Ice reinforces everything. Frozen walls are slippery and unpredictable. One well-placed shot can slide through three layers. Precision replaces brute force.

• Final Realm (Levels 81–100) — The Queen's captors built their most fortified strongholds here. Every skill you've developed is tested. The final levels are not forgiven.

**UPGRADE YOUR KING**

Progress earns coins. Coins upgrade your King's four core stats:

• Power — hit harder, break tougher blocks, punch through reinforced sections
• Speed — faster launch velocity for reaching distant or elevated targets
• Smash Radius — wider area-of-effect destruction per impact
• Armor — the King survives more collisions before a level resets

Find the upgrade path that fits your playstyle. A high-Power build demolishes front walls fast. A high-Smash-Radius build triggers chain collapses across the entire castle. Mix them your way.

**5 POWER-UPS**

When ordinary destruction is not enough, power-ups are ready:

• Bomb Smash — the King detonates on impact, clearing everything in a wide radius
• Fire Smash — burning trajectory sets wooden structures alight before the King even lands
• Ice Smash — freezes the first wall the King strikes, making it brittle for secondary hits
• Lightning Smash — releases a chain of electrical arcs across connected blocks
• Mega King — doubles the King's size and mass for one unstoppable hit

Power-ups are earned through gameplay and available as optional purchases for players who want them ready when it matters most.

**FEATURES**

• 100 hand-crafted levels with 3-star scoring
• Physics-based destruction — every level is solvable in multiple ways
• 6 material types: Wood, Stone, Sandstone, Volcanic Rock, Ice, and Reinforced Steel
• 4 upgradeable King stats with meaningful progression depth
• 5 unique power-ups, each with a distinct destruction mechanic
• 5 distinct worlds with different visual themes and physics environments
• Queen rescue narrative arc across all 100 levels
• Daily rewards system — log in every day for coins and power-ups
• Daily missions — fresh objectives every 24 hours for bonus progression
• Achievement system — 30+ achievements tracking destruction, precision, and completion milestones
• Cloud save — your progress syncs across devices automatically
• Optional Google Sign-In for guaranteed cross-device continuity
• Designed for all skill levels — accessible controls, physics that reward mastery

**FREE TO PLAY**

King Smash is free to download and free to play from the first level to the last. All 100 levels and all gameplay content are accessible without spending anything.

Optional in-app purchases are available for players who want extra coins, gems, or to remove interstitial ads. Rewarded video ads are entirely voluntary — watch one when you choose to for bonus coins or an extra life. Interstitial ads appear between levels and can be permanently removed with a one-time purchase.

No loot boxes. No randomised purchases. No pay-to-win mechanics. Upgrades and power-ups earned through gameplay perform identically to purchased equivalents.

## 7. App Icon — Artist Specification

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| **Dimensions** | 512 × 512 px |
| **Format** | PNG |
| **Color mode** | RGB (not CMYK) |
| **Alpha channel** | Not required — Google Play applies its own rounded-corner mask |
| **Safe zone** | Key content within center 382 × 382 px (75%) — corners will be masked by the Play Store rounded rectangle |
| **Maximum file size** | 1 MB |

### Design direction

The icon must be legible at 48 px (home screen launcher) through 512 px (store hero). Prioritize: clear subject matter, bold contrast, instant genre read.

- **Foreground:** The King character, front-facing, confident expression. Full body or bust (shoulders up). Crown clearly visible. Armor or royal attire matching in-game style.
- **Background:** Enemy castle silhouette, crumbling or cracked, conveying destruction. Dark gradient sky or fiery atmosphere to contrast the King.
- **Color palette:** Bold warm tones for the King (gold, orange, red accents). Cool dark tones for the castle background. High contrast edge between foreground and background.
- **Style:** Cartoon / stylised illustration matching in-game art direction. Clean line art at full size; legible as flat shapes at 48 px.
- **Text on icon:** Not recommended. The app name is shown below the icon on all surfaces. If included, limit to "SMASH" or a crown symbol only — verify at 48 px.
- **Do not include:** Play Store badge imagery, competitor IP, photographic texture that blurs at small sizes, black-only background (disappears against dark phone themes).

**Deliverables from artist:**
- `icon_512x512.png` — final production asset
- `icon_foreground_512x512.png` — foreground layer only (for adaptive icon, if requested)
- `icon_background_512x512.png` — background layer only (for adaptive icon, if requested)

- **Title treatment:** "King Smash" in bold game logo treatment, centered or upper-third. Legible at banner display size (approximately 300 px wide on a phone screen). Use the same typographic style as the in-game title.
- **Tagline (optional):** "Launch. Destroy. Rescue." in a smaller weight below the title.
